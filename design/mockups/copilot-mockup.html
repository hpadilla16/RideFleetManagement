DESIGN · AGENT COPILOT · RIDE UNIVERSITY

Ask, then walk me through it — the copilot that ends "how do I…?"

A chat panel that answers any "how do I…" from the knowledge Ride already ships — and then offers Te enseño: it hands the person to the existing guided-tour engine, which navigates to the exact screen, spotlights the exact button, and walks them step by step until the thing is done.

Nothing here invents machinery. Answers come from the 16 bilingual Ride University articles (backend/src/modules/knowledge-base/default-articles.js) plus the ~35 playbooks on /knowledge-base. "Te enseño" dispatches the production tour event ride-university:start (TourHost.jsx:41) with a moduleKey from curriculum.js — the spotlight, cross-route navigation, record parking and completion verification all already exist and are live. The panel mounts beside TourMount in app/layout.js, so it survives navigation the same way the tour does.

01 · The launcher — quiet until asked

A single dark pill, bottom-right, on every staff screen. It never auto-opens, never bounces, never badges. At the counter the agent's screen belongs to the customer in front of them — the copilot earns its space only when summoned (click or ? key). It renders nothing at all on customer-facing surfaces and while the screen lock is up, exactly like TourMount renders nothing without a cached staff user.

"¿Cómo añado un conductor adicional?" The copilot answers in the asker's language with the exact steps, the source it read them from, and the one mistake that costs money — then offers the two verbs that matter: Te enseño (start the guided walkthrough) and Llévame (just navigate me there). It explains and it guides. It never presses the buttons itself — and before it teaches on a record, it asks which one.

03 · "Te enseño" — the handoff to the tour engine

After the agent taps Sí, aquí on the pre-flight question, the panel collapses to a teal chip and the existing TourHost takes the screen: scrim with a hole, purple spotlight, step card — pixel-for-pixel the production overlay. If the walkthrough lives on another screen, the copilot has already said "Te llevo a … primero" — the navigation itself is TourHost's own step.route push; the copilot only adds the warning. When the walkthrough ends, ride-university:module-walked fires and the chip re-opens with "¿Lo lograste?".

05 · Guardrails — what the copilot will never do
Never acts No clicking, no form-filling, no API writes. Its only two effects on the app are router.push() and dispatching the tour event. The agent's hands do every step — that's also how the training points stay honest (OPPORTUNISTIC modules complete only when the backend sees the real record).
Never invents Answers are retrieved, sourced, and linked. No match → the "doesn't know" state with a KB-search handoff. Unanswered questions are logged per tenant — the miss list is the article backlog.
Always bilingual The corpus is already bilingual in one body; the copilot answers in the language you asked in and the EN/ES toggle re-renders the same sourced answer, not a machine translation.
Respects role & gates Intent matches filter through modulesFor(viewer) — the same role + tenant-module gating the tour uses. An agent asking about Settings gets the honest answer: "that needs an admin", not a tour they can't finish.
