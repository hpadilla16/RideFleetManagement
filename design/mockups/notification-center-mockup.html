Notification center

One bell, one tab, every alert. Today nine surfaces each own a polling loop, a dedupe strategy and a dismissal rule of their own — the center aggregates them into a single severity-ranked feed with per-user read state and team-visible acknowledge, and deep-links back to the surface that owns the work. It replaces nothing in v1.

Design only. Sources cited in the NOTES inventory: overdue-geofence alerts, TollBridge staff alerts, shuttle zone/no-show alerts, kiosk escalations, maintenance due, registration & document expiry, billing dunning, issue-center urgency, plus Feature A's check-in maintenance decisions. The envelope table, per-user read state and the bell are NEW.

Behavior notes — bell

  • Bell sits in the topbar between search and the location picker — the only spot every staff screen shares. Badge = unread for this user within their location scope, capped at 99+.
  • Panel is a skim surface: newest 15, severity icon + one-line title + one-line context + relative time. Unread rows tint --p-50 with a brand dot; click = mark read + deep-link.
  • "Mark all read" clears the personal badge only — it never acknowledges work items on behalf of the team.
  • The shuttle banner (guest physically waiting) keeps its own strip above content — a bell must never be the only path to a human standing at a counter. The center records it; the banner interrupts.

Behavior notes — center

  • Aggregates, never replaces (v1). Every row deep-links to the surface that owns the work — the tolls tray, the maintenance due list, the kiosk queue, the issue. Acknowledge in the center calls the source's existing endpoint where one exists (geofence dismiss, toll ack); it never invents a parallel resolution state.
  • Three severities, fixed meanings. Critical = a human or asset is exposed right now (red, also badges the bell). Needs action = work exists with an owner and a deadline (amber). Informational = awareness only (blue/green, never badges). Sources map into these — severity is in the envelope, not per-surface CSS.
  • Read ≠ acknowledged. Read is per-user (clears the bell, tints the row). Acknowledge is per-tenant and shows who ("Acknowledged by M. Ortiz · 1:44 PM") — the same two-plane model the toll tray (staffAckAt) and geofence alerts (DISMISSED) already use separately, unified.
  • Scoping: events carry tenantId + locationId; the feed applies the caller's effectiveLocationIds like every other module. Role-gated categories (billing → ADMIN) filter at the API, not in the client.
  • Lanes are saved filters, not inboxes — same rail pattern as the tolls redesign, so staff learn it once.
  • Muted rules tab: per-user mutes by category + location ("no shuttle info at Condado"), never mutable for critical.
  • Delivery settings tab (post-MVP): per-category email/SMS digest — the existing per-surface email fan-outs (toll alertEmail, shuttle recipients) migrate here eventually.
