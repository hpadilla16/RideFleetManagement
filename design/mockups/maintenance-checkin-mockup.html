Maintenance detection at check-in

The odometer the agent just typed is the trigger: as soon as Step 3's return mileage crosses a service interval, the wizard says which maintenance is due, how far over, and offers one decision — send the car to the maintenance pool when the return completes, or snooze until the vehicle's next rental event. No new screen; the banner lives inside the existing 6-step check-in wizard.

Design only. Rules are the shipped ServiceSchedule engine (evalSchedule, mileage-driven; miles decide when a mileage basis exists, days only otherwise; "soon" = within 500 mi / 14 days). "Send to maintenance" = open a Repair Order (source SCHEDULED) after check-in close — the RO open flips the vehicle to IN_MAINTENANCE, a locked status the reservation sync never clobbers. The snooze stamp + per-vehicle re-prompt marker are NEW backend.

Behavior notes — state 1

  • The banner mounts inside Step 3, directly under the odometer field it reacts to — cause above effect. It re-evaluates on every keystroke using the schedules already fetched for this vehicle; nothing is written until check-in close.
  • Every schedule that is overdue or due-soon at the typed reading is listed — the agent sees which maintenance, the interval, the baseline, and the gap in the schedule's own basis (miles here; days for date-based rules).
  • The step's Continue is gated while the decision is "pending": the agent must either arm "Send to maintenance" or snooze (state 2). Seeing the banner is not enough — the gate is what makes "the agent saw it" true.
  • One primary action, one consequence sentence. The destructive-adjacent fact (out of the rentable pool) is stated in words, not implied by color.
  • Due-soon rows ride along informationally (amber) — they don't gate, but they're pre-checked into the RO if the agent sends the car in, so the shop does both in one visit.

Behavior notes — state 2

  • Snoozing is legitimate — a checked-in car may be pre-assigned to the next renter. The path costs one click: a single confirm with the re-prompt rule spelled out. No mandatory reason, no presets.
  • Snooze semantics: no re-prompt for the remainder of this check-in; the banner re-surfaces at the vehicle's next check-out or check-in wizard (whichever comes first), recomputed at that event against the then-current odometer — not a timer.
  • The stamp (who / reservation / odometer / when) is recorded silently on snooze — the audit trail survives, at zero extra cost to the agent. The stamp + per-vehicle snooze marker are NEW backend.
  • The overdue item does not disappear from /maintenance's due list — the due list never snoozes.
  • The optional note stays collapsed by default; when written, it shows on the Maintenance hub next to the snooze stamp.

Behavior notes — state 3

  • The action is armed, not fired, at Step 3 — the vehicle is still ON_RENT until close, and the shipped RO-open transition only flips AVAILABLE/RESERVED cars. Sequencing at close: check-in sync sets the car AVAILABLE, then the queued RO opens and locks it IN_MAINTENANCE.
  • Undo works any time before Step 5's signature; the success step then states the outcome in pool language ("out of the rentable pool"), names the RO, and deep-links it.
  • If the RO-open fails at close, check-in still completes (money first); the wizard surfaces "couldn't move to maintenance — open manually" with a retry link rather than blocking the customer.
