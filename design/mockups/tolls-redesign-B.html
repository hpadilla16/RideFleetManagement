Tolls Module · Direction B

The review workbench

Direction B optimizes the thing counter staff actually do 200 times a day: decide one toll, hit one key, move on. The left pane is an ultra-compact list grouped by reservation — because tolls arrive in runs, and eight $1.40 crossings on the same contract are one decision, not eight. The right pane is the evidence: identifier comparison, the score arithmetic, and the toll plotted against the rental window. Hands never leave the keyboard: J/K to move, C confirm, D dispute, W waive.

All existing functions are here: the six views live in the segmented control, search / status / Review-only / Refresh / Export CSV / Auto-Match All / Confirm All in the toolbar, and Confirm / Reset / Dispute / Waive / View as full-size buttons in the evidence pane. Imports & sync and Provider setup use the same secondary-tab pattern as Direction A.

Direction B — what changed and why

Group by reservation

  • Tolls arrive in runs: one renter crosses the same plazas daily. Grouping turns 201 rows into ~60 decisions; "Confirm 3" on a group is one click that today takes three prompts.
  • The group header carries what staff actually scan for: customer, unit, window, total dollars, and the group's weakest confidence — the honest number to judge a batch by.
  • The unmatched bucket sits at the bottom as its own "group" — the shape tells you it's data-entry work, not judgment work.

Evidence, not adjectives

  • The right pane replaces the raw currentVehicleId,plate,… string with three flat cards: identifier comparison, the score ledger with real points (+70/+25/−10), and the toll plotted on the rental window.
  • Naming the losing candidate ("previous renter, window ended before this toll") answers the exact doubt that makes staff hesitate — it's why RES-119005 got mis-billed.
  • No rings, no animation, no glass: the score is a number, the bar is 5px, the timeline is a rectangle. Theater stays out.

Throughput mechanics

  • J/K/C/D/W keyboard loop — the INNOVATION-REVIEW interaction, made concrete. Action bar buttons are 48px; list rows are 40px; nothing below 11px type.
  • All six views, search, status, Review-only, Refresh, Export CSV, Auto-Match All, Confirm All preserved in the toolbar; Imports & sync + Provider setup as secondary tabs (same pattern as Direction A, screen 2).
  • Dispute/Waive open a proper note dialog (not window.prompt); Dispute still creates the Issue Center case.
