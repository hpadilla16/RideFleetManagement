View Payments · Gateway-Aware Redesign

Collect / Record / History

The page splits into the two jobs the counter actually does — make money move (gateway actions, capability-gated) and record what happened (always available) — with history promoted to a first-class audit surface. Every control is conditioned on the tenant's paymentGatewayConfig: an iPOS+SPIn tenant like IRC sees zero Authorize.Net furniture; an Authorize.Net tenant keeps every current function, reorganized. Nothing is deleted — only conditioned and re-homed.

Bilingual note: every label ships with a Spanish string of comparable length (Collect the balance→Cobrar el balance, Send payment link→Enviar enlace de pago, Release hold→Liberar retención, Record a payment→Registrar un pago). Buttons and chips carry ~30% slack; nothing depends on a one-word label. Full EN/ES copy table in view-payments-NOTES.md.

Design annotations

Why the split fixes the brief

  • A (modern): one snapshot band, two labeled zones, one history surface — replacing 9 interleaved blocks and duplicate totals (page.js:530-604).
  • B (tenant-aware): every gateway control keys off a new booleans-only GET /api/settings/payment-capabilities. IRC (gateway:'ipos') renders Screen 1 — zero Auth.Net text, buttons, or background polling.
  • The processor chip in the header tells support staff at a glance why this tenant's page looks the way it does.

Nothing deleted — conditioned

  • All 12 existing functions survive (matrix in NOTES §2): OTC record, list, reconcile, CIM charge/hold/release/save-card, SPIn charge/release/re-auth, refund, void.
  • The AuthNet transId tip becomes a disclosure ("Find a missing payment") instead of a permanent paragraph.
  • Row actions collapse to one ⋯ overflow with consequence copy — "Refund to card" vs "Record refund (no card movement)" mirrors the backend's reference-prefix routing.
  • Spin tools keep their reservation-evidence gate AND gain the tenant gate — legacy reservations stay serviceable.

Rules carried from the tolls redesign & Hector's standing constraints

  • Flat surfaces, brand #8752FE, tabular-nums on all money, 40px touch targets, 11px floor, ~30% ES slack.
  • One primary action per context; destructive actions live behind the overflow with admin labeling.
  • Honest truncation on gateway refs with a copy button; processor chips instead of raw prefixes.
  • Capability locks render once per group with a visible hint (iPad: no hover). Recording a payment is never locked.
  • Themed inline reason/amount fields replace window.prompt/confirm on the money path.
