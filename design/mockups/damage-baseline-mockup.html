Design exploration · Damage baseline · extends the check-in audit

The car's damage story, before any customer touches it

A per-vehicle damage baseline so the post-check-in AI audit never flags pre-existing damage — and never attributes it to the wrong customer. Built on the ledger RFM already has (VehicleDamageReport, HARD_APPROVED = open, FIXED on repair-order complete): this design gives it the missing inflows (audit dismiss fork, onboarding walk-around), feeds it to the photo AI as context, and shows it to the renter at checkout — "you're not responsible for these."

Four mocks: 1 the baseline ledger on the vehicle profile · 2 the two-verb dismiss fork in the audit review pane · 3 the customer-facing known-damage disclosure at checkout (EN + ES) · 4 the onboarding walk-around and the 100-car cold start. Same visual family as the audit + tolls mockups. Design only — no application code.

What is it?
Recorded by M. Rivera · Aug 27, 17:52
Why two verbsThe approved audit design had one dismiss. "The AI saw a shadow" is a false positive; "the AI saw real-but-old damage" means the AI was right and the baseline was incomplete. Only the second one should teach the system about the car.
Where it goesThe pre-existing path calls the existing manual-damage create (view from the flagged angle, dot defaulted to the AI region's center, check-in photo attached) → a HARD_APPROVED ledger entry, source AUDIT_PREEXISTING, reviewer stamped.
Never silentWhen the AI itself matches a flag to a known entry (verdict KNOWN_DAMAGE), the flag is not hidden — it lands in a "Matches baseline" lane with the entry beside it, and confirming it stamps the entry's last-seen date for free.
