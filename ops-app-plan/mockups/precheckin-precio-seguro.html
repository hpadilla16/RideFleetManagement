Pre-check-in: how the customer sees an insurance plan price

Drawn in the customer portal's own tokens (#6e49ff, PortalFrame card/row styles) — not the RideOps/RFM staff theme. Every number below is one the server can compute today; nothing is invented in the browser.

0. What the screen does today (the defect)

Reservation: 5 days, rental before taxes $300.00. Plan "Full Protection" is a PERCENTAGE plan at 10%.

Full Protection
$10.00
Covers collision damage to the rental vehicle.
The customer reads "$10.00" and is charged $30.00. page.js:655 does Number(plan.total || plan.amount || plan.rate || 0) and prints it as money. For a PERCENTAGE plan plan.amount is the percent. The percentage, the base and the real total appear nowhere. A PER_DAY plan has the same shape of bug: it shows the daily rate as if it were the trip total. Root cause is upstream — precheckin-catalog.js:39 never reads the plan's mode, while the charge path (customer-portal.routes.js:1566) reads plan.chargeBy || plan.mode and prices all three modes correctly.

1. Proposed — the three modes

One rule governs the whole row: the money slot only ever contains money the server computed. A rate is never rendered with a dollar sign in the money slot. Under the name, one line of breakdown — the rate, and the base it applies to.

Mode: PERCENTAGE — selected Mode: PER_DAY Mode: FIXED
Your rental before taxes = the car, extras and fees already on your reservation. It does not include taxes or the security deposit.
Why only one line of breakdown. This is someone on a phone accepting a charge, not reconciling an invoice. The three questions a customer actually has are "how much", "why that much", and "on top of what" — rate, base, total answers all three in one line. An itemised sub-table of the base (car + extras + fees) is the fourth question and it belongs in the reservation summary the page already renders, not inside a radio row. FIXED keeps a breakdown line ("One-time charge") because the most common question on a fixed plan is "is that per day?" — two words kill it, and the three rows stay visually parallel.
Why the base is named "your rental before taxes". "Total" is a lie (it is not the final total), "subtotal" is accounting jargon, and bare "your rental" leaves the customer unsure whether tax is in it. "Before taxes" is the one qualifier that changes a decision, and it is literally what the server computes: selected charges minus INSURANCE, minus TAX, minus DEPOSIT (customer-portal.routes.js:1548-1552). The card note spells out the composition once, for the row that wants it, without repeating it per plan.

2. With the pre-check-in discount

Blocking finding — the savings figure is not trustworthy today. makeDiscountFn (precheckin-catalog.js:21) subtracts a FIXED discount from amount. On a PERCENTAGE plan that turns 10% into 5% for a "$5 off" promo — on a $300 base that is $15 off, not $5. The charge path repeats it (customer-portal.routes.js:1585 computes base × discountedAmount/100). So the banner promises $5, the row would claim "You save $5.00", and the ledger moves $15. The strike-through and the "You save" line must both be rendered from server money (computedAmount − discountedComputedAmount), never recomputed in the page — and the FIXED-on-PERCENTAGE semantics has to be decided before this row can claim anything at all.
Struck price recoloured. The current #94a3b8 strike measures 2.33:1 — it is price information, not decoration, and it fails badly. Replaced with #6e6188 at 5.11:1, which keeps the de-emphasis without dropping below AA.

3. When the server does not send the base (today's reality)

This is the state that must never regress into "$10.00".

PERCENTAGE, base unknown PER_DAY, trip length unknown
The decision: show the rate as a rate, never the total as a guess. Three options were on the table. Omit the breakdown and show only a total is impossible — there is no honest total without the base; that is exactly today's bug. Show the percentage alone with no explanation leaves "10%" floating with no referent and invites the customer to read it as $10 anyway. The chosen option keeps the number in its own unit (10%, $12.00 per day — with the unit as the caption, not a dollar sign in the money slot) and states plainly that the amount is still coming. It is the only variant that cannot be misread as money. The amber note is not decoration: a customer must not accept a charge believing the figure shown is the charge — this is the same "subject to approval" disclosure pattern already canon on this page (page.js:722), reused rather than reinvented.
This state should be temporary, not permanent. Everything needed already exists at the GET endpoint — existingCharges and the reservation dates are both in scope at customer-portal.routes.js:1294. Shipping the fallback alone is honest but weak: the customer still cannot compare plans by price. The backend fields in section 6 are the real fix; the fallback is the safety net for plans whose base genuinely cannot be resolved.

4. Mobile (360px) — the surface that matters

Breakdown is its own full-width row, not part of the price column. At 360px a right-aligned price column that also carried the breakdown would squeeze the plan name to two or three words. Keeping name+price on row 1 and breakdown on row 2 means neither ever truncates. The caption shortens to "Total added" under 400px. Whole row stays the tap target at ≥56px tall (target ≥44px); the 18px radio is never the only thing you can hit.

All of it is already in scope at customer-portal.routes.js:1265-1299. Nothing new needs loading.

Must be one shared helper, not a second copy of the math. Extract the base reduce + mode switch into a function called by both the GET (display) and the POST (charge). Two implementations of "what will we charge" is how the quote and the charge drift apart — which is the bug we are fixing, one layer up.
Prerequisite — the GET payload cannot compute the base correctly today. The POST filters the base by chargeType !== 'TAX' and !== 'DEPOSIT' (:1550-1551), but the GET's existingCharges select (:1296) does not include chargeType. Compute the base from today's payload and you quote a base that includes taxes and the deposit, then charge a different one. chargeType: true must be added to that select in the same change.
Frontend, correspondingly: delete the plan.total || plan.amount || plan.rate fallback (page.js:655) — a fallback chain across three fields with different units is what let this through — and delete the client-side discount recomputation (page.js:656-660), which is a third copy of the pricing rules living in the browser.
On the branch that changes the base by no longer netting credits. That branch makes the same plan quote higher. With baseAmount on screen the change is visible to the customer and reviewable by Hector; without it, the number silently moves and nobody outside the diff can see it. These two changes should land together, base-display first.

8. If Hector rejects a change — what happens

Reject: showing the base ("$300.00") in the row. → The percentage plan can only show "10% of your rental before taxes" with no figure. The bug of printing a percent as money is still fixed, but the customer cannot know the amount before accepting, and the branch that changes the base moves the price invisibly. Weakest acceptable outcome.
Reject: the "Total added to your rental" caption. → The bare number returns to being ambiguous — customers read a plan price as the new total of the whole rental. Breakdown line survives and carries most of the meaning; ambiguity moves from "how much" to "on top of what".
Reject: the fallback rate-as-a-rate state (section 3). → There is no honest alternative. The only remaining options are printing the percent as money — today's defect — or hiding percentage plans entirely, which removes coverage the customer may need. If this is rejected, the backend fields in section 6 become mandatory, not recommended, because the fallback can no longer exist.
Reject: the backend fields (computedAmount, baseAmount, mode, days). → Every plan renders in the section-3 fallback forever. Honest, but the customer cannot compare plans on price and the pre-check-in upsell loses most of its purpose. Not a shippable end state, only a safe intermediate.
Reject: fixing the FIXED-discount-on-PERCENTAGE-plan bug now. → The strike-through and the "You save" line must be removed from PERCENTAGE plans entirely, because the amount the banner promises is not the amount the ledger moves. We cannot print a savings claim we know to be wrong. Percentage plans would show a single price with no discount treatment until it is fixed.
Reject: recolouring the struck price to #6e6188. → Price information ships at 2.33:1, unreadable in sunlight on a phone — the exact context this screen lives in. The customer sees only the discounted price and cannot verify the saving.
Reject: the Spanish column / defer portal i18n. → EN ships, ES is filed. No regression — the portal is English-only today — but a Spanish-speaking customer keeps accepting a charge in a second language. Should be a deliberate deferral, not a silent one.
