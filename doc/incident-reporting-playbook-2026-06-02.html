How to file an Incident / Damage Report

When a vehicle comes back with damage, smoke, excessive dirt, or a policy violation, you build a dispute-ready report — with photos, signed certification, and the agreement clauses that justify the charge. This guide walks you through it, screen by screen.

When do I file one?

File a report whenever you'd charge the customer for something beyond normal use, or want a paper trail in case they dispute it: smoking, burns/stains/tears, body damage, missing fuel, excessive cleaning, tolls, or a late return. If in doubt, file it — a DRAFT costs nothing and you can delete it.

One-time setup (manager): before agents cite clauses, a manager seeds the clause library once at Settings → Agreement Clauses → “Seed default clauses,” then reviews the wording. You only do this once per company.

1 Open the reservation and find “Incident reports”

Go to Reservations, open the rental in question, and scroll to the Incident reports panel (blue stripe, near the bottom of the page).

  2. Title — one plain sentence; this is the headline on the report.
  3. Narrative — what you saw, in your own words. Be factual.
Your typing saves automatically when you click out of a box. No “Save” button needed for these fields.

3 Add the evidence (photos)

This is the most important part. Two ways to add photos:

Add photo

Upload a new photo you just took on your phone or computer.

Pull check-in / check-out

Auto-import the photos already taken during the vehicle’s inspection — the check-out set proves it left clean, the check-in set shows the damage.

VIOLATION = proof of the problem. CONFIRMED = proof the car was fine before (pulled from check-out). Having both is what wins a dispute.

Best practice: Pull check-out first (shows the clean baseline), then Add photo for each new problem. Aim for clear, close-up shots.

4 Cite the agreement clauses

Tick the clauses from your rental agreement that the customer broke. These print on the report and justify the charge.

No clauses showing? The manager hasn’t seeded the library yet — see the one-time setup note above.

5 Certify and issue

When the report is complete, certify it. Issuing locks the report so it can’t be quietly changed — that’s what makes it trustworthy in a dispute.

Certify & issue
  1. Type the certifier’s name and title.
  2. Sign in the box (mouse, trackpad, or finger on a tablet).
  3. Click Certify & issue and confirm. The status flips to 🔒 ISSUED.
Double-check before you issue. Once ISSUED, fields are locked. To change anything you must Revise (Step 7), which creates a fresh copy.

6 Preview & share the PDF

Click Preview / PDF any time to open the finished report in a new tab — then print it or save it as a PDF to email the customer or attach to a dispute.

7 Need to change an issued report? Revise it

You can’t edit a locked report — instead click Revise. That makes a new DRAFT copy (carrying over everything) that you edit and re-issue. The original stays on file, so the history is intact.

Common questions

Pull brought in no photos? The inspection had no photos saved, or they haven’t synced — add them manually with Add photo, or tell IT.

Wrong vehicle on the report? The report follows the reservation’s vehicle. Fix the vehicle on the reservation first (or ask a manager to correct it), then start the report.

Made a report by mistake? If it’s still a DRAFT, just remove it. If it’s ISSUED, leave it — issued reports are part of the record; use Revise if the details were wrong.

Customer disputes the charge later? Open the reservation → Incident reports → the issued report → Preview / PDF, and send that. It already has the photos, clauses, and signature a bank needs.
