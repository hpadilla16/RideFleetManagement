Long-Term (Monthly) Reservations

Monthly lives inside the existing reservation module — a LONG-TERM badge plus a plan panel. Locked decisions: 30-day cycles (tenant setting) · 3,000 mi included @ $0.00 overage (per-rate) · charged refundable deposit · no early-return refund · dunning emails 48h/24h before + every 24h overdue, auto-clear on payment.

1 · Creating a monthly reservation

One new control on the existing create form: a rate-type toggle. Picking "Monthly" swaps the daily rate picker for monthly plan cards.

4 · Dunning — reminders, overdue, auto-clear

Timeline for one cycle (tenant-configurable; defaults shown)
Jul 8 · 10:00 AM (48h before)“Your next 30-day rental payment of $1,150.00 bills Jul 10” → email to customer
Jul 9 · 10:00 AM (24h before)Second reminder email
Jul 10 · 10:00 AM (due)Auto-charge card on file → PAID → receipt email. Done.
If the charge fails: grace 1 day → reservation flags PAYMENT OVERDUE, staff alerted, customer emailed every 24h with a pay-by-phone/counter note
The moment payment lands (auto-retry, card-on-file via staff, or OTC at counter) → status auto-clears to CURRENT, overdue emails stop immediately

6 · Customer emails — all four templates

Every template is tenant-configurable (section 7). Merge tags fill from the plan + reservation.

Your 30-day rental of the {{vehicle}} renews on {{dueDate}}. We'll automatically charge {{amount}} to your {{cardBrand}} ending {{cardLast4}} — no action needed.

Planning to return the vehicle instead? Just reply to this email or call us at {{locationPhone}} before {{dueDate}}.

Thank you,
{{companyName}}

Subject: Receipt — {{amount}} · rental renewed through {{periodEnd}}

We received {{amount}} on your {{cardBrand}} ending {{cardLast4}}. Your rental of the {{vehicle}} is paid through {{periodEnd}}.

Thank you,
{{companyName}}
Email C — payment failed / overdue (sent after grace period, repeats every 24h until paid)
Subject: Action needed — payment for your rental could not be processed

Hi {{customerFirstName}},

We were unable to charge {{amount}} for your rental of the {{vehicle}}, due {{dueDate}}.

Pay {{amount}} now → {{payLink}}

Paying online takes 2 minutes and the card you use becomes your new card on file for future renewals. You can also call us at {{locationPhone}} or stop by the counter — we'll keep retrying your current card automatically as well.

If payment isn't received, your rental cannot be renewed past {{periodEnd}}.

{{companyName}}
⚙ The moment ANY payment lands (auto-retry, card-on-file, or counter), the overdue flag clears and these stop immediately — per Hector's spec.
