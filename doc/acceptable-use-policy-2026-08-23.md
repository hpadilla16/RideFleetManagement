# Acceptable Use Policy

**Owner:** [org] · **Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 2026-08-23 · **Review:** annually. **Applies to:** all
staff, administrators and contractors with access to RFM systems or data.

---

## 1. Purpose

Defines the acceptable use of RFM systems, accounts and data, so access is used only for legitimate
business purposes and personal data is protected.

## 2. Rules

- **Use access only for your role** and for legitimate business purposes. Do not access customer data
  you have no business reason to view.
- **Protect your credentials.** Do not share accounts or passwords. **Two-factor authentication is
  required** for privileged accounts. Report a suspected credential compromise immediately.
- **Devices** used to access production must have a screen lock, disk encryption and up-to-date
  software (the Vanta agent verifies this on admin laptops).
- **No data exfiltration.** Do not copy, export or transmit personal data outside approved systems and
  purposes. Do not paste customer personal data into unapproved third-party tools.
- **No unauthorised software or changes** to production; follow the change-management policy.
- **Report** security concerns, suspected incidents, or lost/stolen devices to the incident-response
  contact without delay.

## 3. Monitoring

System and administrative access is logged (request logs + the security audit trail). Use of RFM
systems is subject to monitoring for security and compliance.

## 4. Consequences

Violations may result in revoked access and disciplinary action, up to termination, and legal action
where warranted.

## 5. Acknowledgement

Each person acknowledges this policy on onboarding and annually thereafter (tracked in the readiness
platform).
