Section 3 · What a rental-only employee sees

Rental-only is the stricter mirror: besides data filtering, the Loaner module disappears from the nav entirely (the loaner operation only makes sense inside the loaner program). Loaner-only employees keep their normal modules — just with filtered data — but rental-only additionally loses the Loaner surface.
