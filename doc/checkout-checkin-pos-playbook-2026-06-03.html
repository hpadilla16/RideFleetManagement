Checkout & Check-In with the POS Terminal

The new 6-step checkout wizard, the check-in wizard with live fee preview, and how to record transactions manually when the terminal or card-on-file isn't available.

Applies to the new Spin checkout (v0.9.0-beta.104) Deposit = second tap on terminal (card-on-file pending Dejavoo)

Current mode: the security deposit is taken as a second tap on the terminal. "Charge Card On File" (charging a saved card without the customer present) is not available yet — Dejavoo is enabling it on our merchant account. Until then, any post-rental charge must be taken at the terminal with the customer's card, or recorded manually (Part 3).

1 Checkout (renting the vehicle out)

1 Confirm2 Terms 3 Payment4 Inspection 5 Metrics6 Sign

Step 1 · Confirm customer + vehicle

Open the reservation → Start Checkout. Verify the customer name, phone, vehicle (year/make/model/plate), pickup and return times. If the customer declines rental insurance, tick the Declined Insurance box — it adds an acknowledgement section they'll initial in Step 2.

Step 2 · Terms & Conditions

The customer signs the T&C on their own phone — have them scan the QR code on your screen. The wizard advances automatically when they finish. If their phone won't cooperate, they can sign on your screen.

Step 3 · Payment — two taps

The wizard shows the rental balance (e.g. $312.40) and the security deposit (e.g. $250.00) separately. They are charged separately:

  1. Tap 1 — Sale. Press Charge on terminal. The terminal wakes up and prompts the customer to tap/insert. This charges the rental balance and saves the card on file for later.
  2. Tap 2 — Deposit hold. After the sale approves, press the deposit hold button. The terminal prompts for a second tap. This places a hold (authorization) — it is NOT a charge; it falls off automatically if released at check-in.

Pre-paid customers (balance $0.00): tap 1 is skipped automatically. One single tap captures the card on file and places the deposit hold.

Tell the customer: "The first tap is your rental charge. The second tap is the deposit — it's a hold, not a charge, and it's released when you bring the car back."

Terminal down or card processed another way? Use Record manually — see Part 3.

Step 4 · Inspection handoff

Scan the QR with the lot phone/tablet to run the walk-around inspection (photos, damage marks) at the vehicle. The wizard advances when the inspection is submitted.

Step 5 · Metrics

Record the odometer and fuel level going out. These are what check-in fees get measured against — be accurate.

Step 6 · Customer signature → Done

The customer signs the final agreement. The wizard closes, the reservation flips to CHECKED_OUT, and the vehicle goes ON RENT. The agreement PDF is emailed automatically.

2 Check-In (vehicle comes back)

Step 1–2 · Open check-in + inspect

Open the reservation → Start Check-In. Run the return inspection (photos, new damage). New damage → also file an Incident Report (see the Incident Reporting playbook).

Step 3 · Metrics with live fee preview

Enter the return odometer, fuel level, cleanliness, and smoking check. The panel on the right shows the fees building live as you type — fuel shortage, cleaning, smoking, and late-return fees, using the rates from Settings → Inspection Fees. What you see is exactly what the system will charge.

  • Waive Late Return Fee — agent override for courtesy / flight delay / authorized overrun. Use judgment; it's logged.

Step 4 · Balance settlement — pick one

OptionWhat happensWhen to use
Autocharge Fees are queued and charged to the card on file after the tenant-configured delay (e.g. 24h). Reservation goes to CHECKED_IN_UNPAID until the charge lands, then CHECKED_IN. Customer is dropping off and leaving (e.g. heading to the airport). NOTE: until Dejavoo enables card-on-file, autocharge attempts will fail — prefer paying now or record manually.
Manual / pay now Take payment at the terminal now (customer taps), or record an OTC payment (cash/external). Reservation goes straight to CHECKED_IN once paid. Customer is present, or any time card-on-file is unavailable.

Step 5 · Signature + deposit release

The customer signs acknowledging the fees. Then release the deposit hold from View Payments → Release Deposit Hold (reason required — e.g. "vehicle returned clean, no damage"). Don't forget this: a forgotten hold is the #1 source of customer callbacks.

3 Manual transaction recording (bypassing the terminal)

Use these when the terminal is offline/frozen, the charge was taken some other way (cash, external machine, phone payment), or card-on-file is unavailable. Every manual entry is logged with your name — always fill in the reference and reason fields.

A · During checkout — Step 3 failsafes

  • Record Manual Payment (next to the sale button): enter amount, method (cash/card/other), reference (receipt #, external terminal auth code), and notes. The wizard advances exactly as if the terminal approved — but no card is saved on file, so later charges for this rental must also be manual.
  • Record Manual Deposit Hold (next to the deposit button): enter amount, method, reference, and a reason (required) — e.g. "terminal offline, cash deposit taken" or "hold run on standalone terminal, auth #123456".
Important: a manual deposit hold is a record, not a real hold. No money is frozen on the customer's card unless you actually ran an authorization on the standalone terminal or took a cash deposit. Never mark a manual deposit "held" if nothing was actually collected or authorized.

B · Any time — View Payments tools

Reservation → View Payments:

  • Record OTC Payment — log an over-the-counter payment (cash, check, external card machine). For card payments enter the last 4 digits. Use this to settle a CHECKED_IN_UNPAID balance — recording the payment moves the reservation to CHECKED_IN.
  • Charge Card On File — charges the saved card without the customer present. Unavailable until Dejavoo enables CNP — if it errors ("Transaction Failed"), collect the payment another way and Record OTC Payment instead.
  • Release Deposit Hold — voids the hold on the customer's card; reason required. Holds tagged MANUAL only update our records (there's no terminal authorization to void) — if you took a cash deposit, hand the cash back and note it in the reason.

C · Golden rules for manual entries

  1. Money first, record second. Only record what was actually collected or authorized.
  2. Always attach a reference — receipt number, standalone-terminal auth code, or check number.
  3. Reasons are mandatory on deposits and releases. Write what a manager would need to reconstruct it tomorrow.
  4. Manual sale = no card on file. Warn the customer that any later fees will need them to pay directly.
  5. If you're not sure, don't guess — pause the wizard and call the manager.
